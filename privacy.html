Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

FaithRank ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the FaithRank mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you create an account and use FaithRank:

  • Email address (for account creation and authentication)
  • Username (chosen by you)
  • Church name (optional, chosen by you)
  • Denomination (chosen by you)
  • Quiz scores and performance data
  • Daily quiz streak and activity data

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display your scores on leaderboards
  • Track your quiz progress and streaks
  • Send daily quiz reminder notifications (if enabled)
  • Improve the app experience

Information Sharing

Your username, church name, denomination, and scores are displayed publicly on leaderboards within the app. Your email address is never shared publicly or sold to third parties.

Third Party Services

FaithRank uses the following third party services:

  • Supabase — for secure data storage and authentication
  • RevenueCat — for managing in-app purchases
  • Expo — for push notification delivery

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which uses industry-standard encryption. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us.

Push Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, we will send you one push notification per day reminding you to complete your daily quiz. You can disable notifications at any time in the app settings or your device settings.

In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed through Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not store your payment information. Purchase history is managed by RevenueCat.

Children's Privacy

FaithRank is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us at:

support@faithrank.app

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
